NexusCompress is a privacy-first image toolkit for everyday use — including preparing photos and documents for common UAE portal upload limits (Emirates ID, MOHRE, ICA, and similar). Everything runs on your device: files never upload to our servers.

**Compress & convert**
• Batch compress JPEG, PNG, WebP, and AVIF  
• One-tap presets for Emirates ID (~200 KB) and MOHRE (~500 KB)  
• HEIC to JPEG, format converter, SVG optimizer  

**Documents & PDF**
• Images to PDF, merge/split PDF, PDF to images  
• PDF to Markdown, document redactor with OCR  

**ID & passport photos**
• Photo compliance checker (10 automated checks)  
• Passport / ID photo studio with print sheet layouts  
• Crop, collage, and AI background removal  

**Advanced (on-device)**
• AI image upscaler (no cloud)  
• Compare before/after, ZIP export, dark mode  

**Why NexusCompress**
✓ 100% client-side — your files stay on your device  
✓ No account required  
✓ Full Arabic (RTL) interface at compress.funadventure.ae/ar  
✓ Free core tools  

Perfect for residents preparing portal uploads, freelancers optimising images, and anyone who wants fast compression without handing photos to a cloud service.

Privacy policy: https://compress.funadventure.ae/privacy.html
