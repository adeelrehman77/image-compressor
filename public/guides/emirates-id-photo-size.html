Emirates ID photo size and file limits

When renewing or applying for an Emirates ID, portals often reject oversized phone photos. This guide covers common size targets and how to prepare a compliant JPEG privately.

Disclaimer: Independent tool by Fun Adventure Media Studio — not affiliated with ICA/MOHRE. Verify limits on official portals. Reviewed 2026-08-11.

How do I compress to 200 KB?

Open NexusCompress, select the Emirates ID / 200 KB preset (or set target 200 KB), drop your file, and download.

Yes. Processing runs in your browser; files are not uploaded to NexusCompress servers for compression.

No. They are commonly reported targets. Always verify on the official portal. NexusCompress is not a government app.
