ICA photo upload requirements (size and format)

ICA and ICA Smart Services uploads frequently require JPEG files within a strict size cap (commonly around 200 KB for photos). Prepare files locally, then verify on the official site.

Open NexusCompress, select the Emirates ID / ICA (~200 KB) preset (or set target 200 KB), drop your file, and download.

Yes. Processing runs in your browser; files are not uploaded to NexusCompress servers for compression.

No. They are commonly reported targets. Always verify on the official portal. NexusCompress is not a government app.
