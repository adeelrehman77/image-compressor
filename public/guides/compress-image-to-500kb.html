Compress an image to 500 KB (MOHRE / labour documents)

MOHRE and many labour-related portals commonly cap document scans around 500 KB JPEG. Use NexusCompress to batch-shrink scans locally before upload.

Disclaimer: Independent tool by Fun Adventure Media Studio — not affiliated with ICA/MOHRE. Verify limits on official portals. Reviewed 2026-08-11.

Steps

  1. Open NexusCompress.
  2. Select MOHRE / labour portal (~500 KB JPEG) or set target size 500 KB.
  3. Drop your photo or scan (use HEIC Converter for iPhone photos first if needed).
  4. Download when under 500 KB and upload to the official portal.

FAQ

How do I compress to 500 KB?

Open NexusCompress, select the MOHRE / labour portal (~500 KB JPEG) preset (or set target 500 KB), drop your file, and download.

Is this private?

Yes. Processing runs in your browser; files are not uploaded to NexusCompress servers for compression.

Are these official ICA/MOHRE limits?

No. They are commonly reported targets. Always verify on the official portal. NexusCompress is not a government app.
