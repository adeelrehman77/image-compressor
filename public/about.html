About NexusCompress

NexusCompress is a privacy-first image and document toolkit that runs entirely in your browser. It is built and operated by Fun Adventure Media Studio.

What we build

NexusCompress helps people compress JPEG, PNG, WebP, and AVIF images; convert HEIC; prepare passport and Emirates ID photos; check photo compliance; redact sensitive scans; merge PDFs; and more — without creating an account.

A major use case is meeting common UAE portal upload size limits (for example ~200 KB for Emirates ID / ICA-style uploads and ~500 KB for many MOHRE document scans). Presets are convenience helpers based on publicly discussed limits; they are not official government settings.

Privacy model

Image and document processing happens on your device (browser canvas, Web Workers, and on-device models where applicable). NexusCompress does not upload your files to our servers for compression or conversion. See the Privacy Policy for analytics and cookies.

Not a government app

NexusCompress is an independent product. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by ICA, MOHRE, RTA, ICP, or any other government entity. Always verify current requirements on official portals such as ica.gov.ae, smartservices.ica.gov.ae, mohre.gov.ae, and u.ae.

Brand disambiguation

NexusCompress at compress.funadventure.ae is this browser toolkit. It is unrelated to other open-source libraries or crates that may share a similar name.
